4) mmu diag test 5 (execute protect) walked through all segments. After jmp (A1) into a no execute segment the opcode fetch at the target faulted with REG_PPC already set to the target, so the format 8 frame held 200000 where a 68010 stacks 3a34, the PC after the jmp. m68ki_read_imm_16/32 now remember where the last instruction stream read ended; when the opcode fetch itself faults and that differs from REG_PPC, m68ki_exception_bus_error stacks it. The target is kept in two of the internal information words, so an rte of that frame with RR clear still completes the jump.

5) svs_fortran, wcc and the other programs with a zero stack size in the header died with "Memory management error" on their first stack probe. The BOSS/IX kernel (0x1904) grows the stack only when the word at frame PC - 2 is tst.b d16(A7) (4a2f) or tst.b (A7) (4a17), and computes the growth from the negated fault address. A 68010 data fault frame holds the PC of the instruction plus 2 and the full 32 bit fault address (ffffe654); Musashi stacked the instruction start and the 24 bit address the host passes to m68k_set_bus_error_address (00ffe654). m68ki_exception_bus_error now keeps the upper address bits of the access, stacks REG_PPC + 2 for faults outside the opcode fetch and keeps REG_PPC in the internal information words, where rte picks it up to restart the instruction. m68ki_rerun_match masks both sides of the address compare, since the rerun address now carries the upper bits.
